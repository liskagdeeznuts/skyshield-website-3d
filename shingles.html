Lomanco Lo-Omni Ridge Vent

A rolled, shingle-over ridge vent with an external baffle and weatherproof joint. It pulls hot, damp air out of the attic across the whole ridge while an external water shield keeps wind-driven rain out.

  • External baffleBoosts exhaust airflow
  • Water shieldResists wind-driven rain
  • 2:12 to 16:12Fits a wide pitch range
  • Shingle-overHidden under the ridge cap

Ice & Water Barrier

A self-adhering, peel-and-stick membrane (TAMKO Moisture Guard®) sealed to the deck at the spots most likely to leak: eaves, valleys, and around anything that pokes through the roof. If water ever gets past the shingles, this is what stops it.

  • Self-adheringSeals around fasteners
  • Eaves & valleysIce-dam and storm protection
  • PenetrationsChimneys, vents, skylights

SkyShield Synthetic Underlayment

A tough, water-resistant synthetic layer (TAMKO Synthetic Guard™ Plus) rolled over the entire roof as the second line of defense under your shingles. Ours is printed with the SkyShield name, so you can see exactly whose work is protecting your home.

  • 20-yearLimited warranty
  • Whole-roofCoverage over the deck
  • SkyShieldCustom-branded imprint

And a sound deck underneath it all.

Every layer is only as good as what it is fastened to. Before anything goes down, we check the plywood or OSB decking and replace anything soft or rotted, so your new roof starts on a solid base.
