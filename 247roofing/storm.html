Why you can't wait

The worst hail damage hides in plain sight.

A 4.75″ stone will punch through a windshield — but on your roof, the damage that voids shingles and starts leaks is often invisible from the ground. It quietly shortens your roof's life and shows up as interior leaks months later, long after the claim window closes and insurers start calling it "wear and tear."

Bruised shingles

Impacts soften the mat beneath the surface. You won't see it from the yard, but the shingle is already failing.

Granule loss

Hail knocks off the granules that block UV. Bare asphalt bakes, cracks, and ages years in months.

Dented metal & gutters

Dinged gutters, vents, and flashing are the tell your adjuster looks for — and the first place water sneaks in.

Delayed leaks

The ceiling stain you'll notice next spring started April 28th. By then, proving the cause is far harder.

The clock is the enemy. Most Missouri policies require prompt notice of storm damage. Document it now, while the cause is clearly the April 28th hail — not next year when it's your word against the adjuster's. A free inspection puts it on record at no cost to you.

The outcome most homeowners don't realize

Your new roof may already be paid for.

When a storm like April 28th damages your roof, a properly documented insurance claim can cover a full replacement — and in most cases you're responsible only for your deductible. The catch is documentation. Miss the damage, under-report it, or wait too long, and money you're owed stays on the table.

  • We inspect and document every hit — photos, measurements, and a written report built the way adjusters expect to see it.
  • We meet your adjuster on the roof so nothing legitimate gets missed or written off during the inspection.
  • We restore it to code — a full, warrantied replacement using quality materials built for Missouri weather.
  • Mismatched shingles? That matters in Missouri. Missouri courts have held replacement materials must be "virtually identical" — so when a patch won't match your roof, that can support replacing the full slope. We document the mismatch for your file.
