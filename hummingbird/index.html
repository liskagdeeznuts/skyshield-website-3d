Good to know

Questions homeowners ask us most.

How much does a new roof cost?
Every roof is different — size, pitch, material, and condition all factor in. That’s exactly why we offer a free inspection and a transparent, itemized quote, so you see real numbers for your home with no obligation.
Do you offer free inspections and quotes?
Yes. Your inspection and quote are always free. We’ll assess your roof, document what we find with photos, and walk you through your options with zero pressure.
What types of roofs do you install?
We work with asphalt shingle, metal, tile, and flat roof systems, plus siding. Not sure which is right for your home? We’ll help you weigh cost, longevity, and looks.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes — we’re fully licensed and insured, and we build every roof to local Middle Tennessee codes. Your home and our team are protected on every job.
How fast can you get to my home?
We typically respond the same day and can often schedule your inspection within a few days. If you’ve got an active leak or storm damage, call us and we’ll prioritize you.
