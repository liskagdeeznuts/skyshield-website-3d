The layers that matter

A roof is a system. We build every layer right.

The shingles are what you see — but it’s the layers underneath that keep water out and your roof standing for decades.

01 — Shingles & panels

The weather-facing layer, in wind- and impact-rated grades, protecting everything below and defining your home’s look.

02 — Ridge ventilation

Lets superheated attic air escape while keeping rain and pests out — cooler attics, lower bills, and a longer roof life.

03 — Ice & water barrier

A peel-and-stick membrane sealing the eaves, valleys, and penetrations — exactly where leaks like to start.

04 — Synthetic underlayment

A tough, water-resistant layer across the whole deck — a second line of defense under your shingles.

05 — Flashing details

Precision metal work at chimneys, walls, and valleys — the small details that make or break a watertight roof.

06 — Solid decking

The structural wood base it all fastens to. We inspect it on every job and replace anything that’s soft or rotten.
