Who we are

A local name we’re proud to stand behind.

Hummingbird Roofing & Siding was built on four principles — integrity, loyalty, quality, and years of experience — and we apply them to every home we work on across Middle Tennessee.

Our story

Founded on principles, run like a promise.

We started Hummingbird because we believe roofing should be simple, honest, and done to a standard we’d want on our own homes. Those aren’t just words on a wall — they’re how we make decisions on the ladder and on the phone.

We stand proudly by our company’s name and reputation. That means straight answers, quality workmanship, and treating your home and your time with respect from the first call to the final cleanup. When the job is done, we want you to feel like you called the right people — because you did.
