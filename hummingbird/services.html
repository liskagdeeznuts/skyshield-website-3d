What we do

Full-service roofing & siding, done right.

From a quick repair to a complete replacement, we handle every roof type across Middle Tennessee — with premium materials, transparent pricing, and a workmanship guarantee behind it.

01 — Roof Replacement

A complete roof system, built to outlast the weather.

When repairs no longer make sense, we replace your roof the right way — a full tear-off down to good decking, then a complete, code-correct rebuild using premium, manufacturer-warrantied materials.

  • Full tear-off, deck inspection, and repair of any damaged wood
  • Ice & water barrier, synthetic underlayment, and proper ventilation
  • Your choice of asphalt, metal, tile, or flat systems and colors
  • Manufacturer warranty plus our own workmanship guarantee
Get a replacement quote

02 — Roof Repair

Fixed to last — not patched to get by.

A leak is rarely just a leak. We track down the real source, explain what we find, and repair it properly so the problem stays fixed. No upselling, no scare tactics.

  • Leak diagnosis, missing or damaged shingles, cracked flashing
  • Valley, chimney, skylight, and vent-boot resealing
  • An honest verdict: repair when it makes sense, replace when it doesn’t
Schedule a repair

03 — Roof Inspections

Know exactly what shape your roof is in.

Our free, photo-documented inspection gives you a clear picture of your roof’s health — so you can plan ahead instead of reacting to the next storm.

  • Thorough top-to-bottom assessment of shingles, flashing, and vents
  • Clear photos of everything we find — you see what we see
  • Straightforward recommendations with zero obligation
Book a free inspection

04 — Siding

Seal and refresh the rest of your home.

Your roof and siding work as a team to keep water out. We install durable, great-looking siding that protects your walls and instantly lifts your home’s curb appeal.

  • Full siding replacement and targeted repairs
  • Weather-tight installation that stands up to Tennessee humidity
  • A wide range of colors and profiles to match your home
Ask about siding
