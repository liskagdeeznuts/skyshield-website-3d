# SkyShield Roofing — Brand Guidelines

> For Claude Code: reference this on anything visual. Colors derived from the SkyShield Command
> Center (SSCC-v.1.2.html) + the live site logo. Voice from the current site copy.

## Company
- **Name:** SkyShield Roofing of New England
- **Tagline:** Your SHIELD Against the Elements.
- **Positioning:** Premium, high-trust CT roofing contractor. Communication is the differentiator.

## Color palette

### Primary
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Royal Blue (logo) | `#004AAD` | Primary brand blue — logo, primary buttons, key accents |
| Brand Blue | `#1D4ED8` | Headings accent, links, secondary buttons |
| Bright Blue | `#3B82F6` | Highlights, icons, focus states, hover |
| Sky Blue | `#60A5FA` | Light accents, brand name text on dark |

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|

### Accents (status / semantic — use sparingly)
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Success Green | `#4ADE80` | "Covered" / completed states |
| Warning Amber | `#FBBF24` | Star ratings, highlights |
| Alert Red | `#F87171` | Damage callouts (use carefully) |

**Default direction:** premium light site with deep-navy hero/footer and blue accents. Use the navy
gradient for the hero and footer; keep body sections airy with generous whitespace (Autarq rhythm).

## Voice
Trustworthy, premium, plain-spoken, homeowner-facing. Education over pressure. Every CTA is a
no-obligation inspection. Lead with communication and craftsmanship, back it with proof (5.0 / 138
reviews, 350 roofs, Top 5% TAMKO). Never promise guaranteed insurance payouts.

## Imagery
Real drone (DJI) roof photography from completed jobs. Crisp before/afters. Human touch (the
thank-you card). Avoid generic stock and "AI vibe" gradients.
