Is the roof inspection really free?

Yes. We get up on the roof, document what we find with photos, and walk you through it. There is no cost and no obligation.

How do I know if I need a new roof?

Look for missing or curling shingles, a sagging roofline, water stains on your ceilings, or leaks that keep coming back. Wind and hail damage is often invisible from the ground, which is why a close inspection matters.

Does homeowners insurance cover roof damage?

It often does when the damage comes from a covered event like wind, hail or falling debris, but coverage depends on your policy. We document the damage, meet your adjuster and handle the back-and-forth. In Connecticut, the matching law can also affect how much of the roof gets replaced.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most homes are done in one to two days, weather permitting. Larger or more complex roofs can take longer, and we tell you up front.

Do I need to be home during the install?

No. As long as we can get to the property, you do not need to be home. We keep you updated the whole way, and you can follow the job on your own progress dashboard.

What warranties do you offer?

As a TAMKO Diamond Pro contractor, we can register TAMKO's enhanced warranties, which add workmanship coverage on top of the shingle warranty. We go over the exact terms for your roof at the inspection.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Lisbon, Connecticut, and serve eastern Connecticut, southern Rhode Island and Eastern Massachusetts, including Norwich, New London, Groton, Mystic, Westerly and Narragansett.
