Privacy policy

SkyShield Roofing of New England ("SkyShield," "we," "us") runs this website. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions? Email sales@skyshieldroofpro.com, call 860-726-4727, or write to 110a River Rd., Lisbon, CT 06351.

What we collect

  • Information you give us. When you request an inspection we collect your name, phone number, email, property address, anything you tell us about your roof, and whether you want text updates. We also keep records of calls, texts and emails you send us.
  • Information collected automatically. We use Google Analytics to understand how people use the site: pages viewed, how you arrived, device and browser type, and approximate location. Google Analytics uses cookies to do this.
  • Project information. If you become a customer, we keep job details such as photos of your roof, inspection findings, insurance claim information you share with us, and your project schedule.

How we use it

  • To respond to you, schedule and perform your inspection, and prepare your estimate.
  • To help with your insurance claim when you ask us to, including meeting your adjuster.
  • To run your project and send you updates, including through your customer progress dashboard.
  • To register manufacturer warranties on the products we install.
  • To improve this website, and to meet our legal and business obligations.

How we share it

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us run our business, such as our website host, form and email tools, Google Analytics, and our customer and project management software. They may use it only to provide services to us.
  • Your insurance company or adjuster, when you ask us to help with a claim.
  • Product manufacturers, such as TAMKO, to register your warranty.
  • Others when required by law, or to protect our rights and safety.

Cookies and analytics

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on. Content embedded from other sites, such as our project map, may set its own cookies under that provider's policy.

Keeping it safe, and for how long

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information and keep it only as long as we need it for the purposes above, including warranty and legal record-keeping.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by emailing sales@skyshieldroofpro.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state law. This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Text messages

If you check the box to receive texts, SkyShield may text you about your inspection, estimate and project. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase. We do not share your mobile number or text messaging consent with third parties for their marketing.
