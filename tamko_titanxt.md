  weathering asphalt layer.

## The 4 "Key Features" (TAMKO's cutaway-diagram callouts)
> These map to the `titanxt-construction-cutaway.png` shingle-overlay graphic — great for an animated
> or numbered breakdown on the Shingles page.

1. **Granules Hold Firm** — Advanced asphalt technology with proprietary additive blends and premium
   material sourcing helps ensure superior granule adhesion for long-lasting protection even in
   extreme conditions.
2. **Sealant Grips Strong** — Next-generation sealant resists high-temperature compression while
   forming a powerful bond that grips strong, even in freezing temperatures.
3. **Laminant Maintains Integrity** — Engineered laminant design locks the shingle system together
   to maintain integrity, even under intense summer heat or steep slopes.
4. **Reinforcement Secures the System** — The proprietary **AnchorLock™** layer reinforces the system
   and helps keep Titan XT® firmly in place against winds up to 160 MPH.

## Technology behind Titan XT®
- **TriShield™ technology** — a three-part system that enhances durability through advanced asphalt
  formulation, premium fiberglass mat, and strong granule adhesion.
- **AnchorLock™ layer** — reinforced base layer engineered for superior wind protection and hold
  strength.
- **Class 3 impact-rated** — built to withstand severe weather, backed by rigorous UL 2218 testing.
- **APEX Color®** — color technology delivering rich, lasting tones across both collections.
- **TAMKO Complete® roof system** — Titan XT pairs with TAMKO starter, hip & ridge, underlayments,
  and accessories for peak performance and Enhanced Warranty eligibility.
